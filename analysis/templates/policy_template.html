Historical DTV Masking Comparison

The deployed mask rejects any frame with positive pilot excess (F > μ0) — the most conservative possible rule. Priced through the CHIME Fisher forecast, that rule masks so much of the DTV band that the z ≈ 1.45 distance bin becomes unmeasurable, and it keeps masking channels whose transmitters died years ago. This historical brief compares it with the fixed report rule F > ημ0, η = 1.4. It does not select or recommend an operational threshold. The report labels channels with no remaining transmitter clean, and labels channels with a live carrier kept-and-masked or excised. The trawl is complete: all 23 channels (14–36) are complete against their archive holdings (the archive itself is thin for the two strongest carriers, ch 24/30); see the companion Pilot Proxy Trawl data report.

The picture

How the historical report rule was constructed

Fraction of current-epoch frames (, every month after the last detected transmitter transition) that each step of the threshold's development masks. Step 1: the idealized rule F > 1, unit null mean (open square). Step 2: F > μ0, the exact per-channel null mean calibrated from the survey's own results (open circle) — the conservative rule we ran with while the tolerance machinery was built; the step-1→2 shift is tens of percent on quiet channels because μ0 sits up to ±1.1% from unity. Step 3: the fixed historical report rule F > 1.4 μ0 from the science-residual pricing (filled dot). Step 4 — selecting each channel's excess threshold ρ̂ = F/μ0 − 1 from its own residual chain — belongs to the current calibration path and is outside this artifact. Channels with a live transmitter stay masked at every report step; the ladder frees only channels labeled clean.

Historical labels

Per-channel report comparison

A “clean” report label applies the fixed η = 1.4 rule; an “excise” label prices the 6 MHz as lost volume. These are historical labels, not operational dispositions. Costs are survey-level integration-time multipliers for a fixed science target, evaluated at the coherence cap (the residual bracket's pessimistic end). μ0 is each channel's exact null mean under its calibrated configuration — calibrated per channel from the survey's own results, then fixed; the exactness is in the arithmetic, the per-channel values are measurements. Kept ρ̂ is the measured pilot excess (F/μ0 − 1, in dB) of the frames the report rule keeps. Per-channel ρ̂ thresholds from each channel's residual chain are selected by the current calibration path, not by this artifact.

Methodology

How the numbers were made

  • Inputs: 21 per-pilot survey products (pilotproxy_detector_datatrawl_v3, one kernel, 2018-12–2026-07, 9,192 events probed, complete against archive holdings), the RFIsher forecast bank (CHIME Overview 2022, Planck-2018; clean survey 5σ = on-sky hours), and residual.threshold_sweep for the contamination side.
  • Window rule: masked fractions are computed from months after the most recent detected transmitter transition (ch 19 sign-off, Dec 2024), i.e. onward. Re-evaluate the window whenever the monthly occupancy map shows a new transition.
  • Threshold fractions are rethresholded from each product's stored statistic (F > ημ0), stamped as such, never read from the shipped mask.
  • Coherence bracket: residual ratios are evaluated at the measured correlation time where the estimator's gates pass, and otherwise at BOTH bracket ends — the sidereal-day cap (pessimistic: flatters masking) and the thermal floor ncoh = 1 (optimistic). A historical label that holds at both ends is reported; where the ends disagree the survey-cost spread is quoted and is <1% in every such case.
  • Sign-off channels forward: kept-frame residual is set to zero for channels with no remaining transmitter; the archival off-epoch floors (−26 to −34 dB shelves) put any true residual far below thermal after ground and delay filtering.
  • Disclosures: channels without a measurable off-epoch floor use the floor_provenance σ-implied substitute (flagged per row). Ch 30 has no current-epoch data because CHIME stopped collecting that channel in Sep 2023 due to the contamination itself — the instrument's own operational exclusion independently corroborates the historical excision label. The same curtailment explains the thin ch 24 archive. After the v1 health gate, channels 22 and 24 have no stored-mask kept/null frame; their floor and sweep are therefore unavailable, and the historical report labels them excised pending independent clean/off evidence rather than claiming a coherence-bracket result.

Remaining evidence and operations

What the completed archive cannot decide by itself

  • The archive trawl is complete: all 23 channels were processed. The 170,377-row source inventory is exactly accounted for by 170,374 product units and three quarantined source units (two truncated HDF5 files and one unreadable object header). No channel remains in a scan queue, and the quarantine does not remove an event from the processed event union.
  • Control-frequency null trawl: one pilot-free freq_id through the same pipeline pins the μ0 null (ch 29 sits 63.7% above μ0 at 0.01 dB) and puts an error bar under every low-η fraction.
  • Burst-channel coherence: the ch 34/36 rows rest on the bracket. The cadence campaigns that would measure τc are specified (a few half-day scheduled runs at ~10 s cadence) but may not be executed; the within-event frame-to-frame coherence already in the archive is the fallback measurement. If neither happens, the sidereal-day caps stand as permanent conservative bounds and certification shifts downstream: blocked holdout, per-channel jackknife in the final science analysis, and the spectral sentinel. Inclusion stays safe because the mask is software — every kept channel can be dropped post hoc; excision is the only irreversible act, which is why it is now applied only where nothing is recoverable.
  • Do not adopt the fixed η=1.4 report rule operationally. The current calibration path must select or refuse per-channel thresholds from its recorded evidence. The archive-health audit’s narrow measured anchors use provisional UTC-quarter evidence and remain retrospective until a prospective anchor/null contract is frozen.
  • Periodic spot-collection on excluded channels: three transmitters died during this survey, but a channel CHIME stops collecting (ch 30 since Sep 2023) can never report its own sign-off. A low-cadence probe — a few baseband dumps per quarter on the excised freq_ids — is cheap insurance against permanently writing off recovered spectrum.
