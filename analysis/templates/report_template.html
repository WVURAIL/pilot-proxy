CHIME/FRB baseband · ATSC pilot survey · status report · 2026-08-18

Pilot Proxy Trawl

Twenty-one CHIME frequency channels, each containing the 8-VSB pilot tone of one ATSC digital-TV channel (physical channels 16–36, 482–602 MHz), trawled across the full CHIME/FRB baseband archive with the pilot-proxy detector. All twenty-one channels are complete against their archive holdings (channels 14–15 are queued in the scan). The processed set spans 7.6 years and resolves the local DTV transmitter history — sign-ons, sign-offs, and warm-season anomalous propagation — channel by channel.

Propagation

Weak channels breathe with the seasons and the clock

Channels 36, 28, and 18 have no dominant local transmitter, yet hold a small persistent excess that peaks in July and mid-morning (Pacific time) — the signature of warm-season tropospheric enhancement of distant stations rather than receiver artifacts.

Each carrier localized to a few hertz

Mean fine-bin F-statistic for the six channels with a strong carrier. The fine reduction is time-coherent: an FFT along the window axis resolves the carrier’s complex envelope into 256 bins of 11.92 Hz spanning ±1,526 Hz around the target coarse bin (verified against fine_reduction.py; the axis is sense-flipped relative to RF). Mask verification

The positive-excess mask removes the pilot line

Integrated 23.8 Hz-resolution spectrum for channel 30 (566.31 MHz pilot), before and after the per-frame mask, each normalized to its own broadband shelf. Frames surviving the mask carry 0.03% of the total power (−35.9 dB) and show no pilot line above the shelf.

Operating point

Where the mask threshold should actually sit

The deployed mask (F > μ0, η = 1) is the most conservative choice. Sweeping the threshold F > ημ0 upward and folding in the residual contamination of the kept frames (baonoise.residual.threshold_sweep) gives the net effective-sensitivity multiplier net = (1+runmasked)/(1+rmasked) × (1−f): above 1 the threshold pays, below 1 it discards more signal than contamination. The best point is marked; sweeps start where enough frames survive. τc is measured where the estimator’s gates pass (ch 35: 46 min, ch 31: 34 min, ch 24: 62 min) and held at the sidereal-day bound elsewhere — the bound maximizes the apparent benefit of masking, so bound-based nets are upper limits. Channels without a measurable off-epoch floor use the floor_provenance σ-implied substitute. The sign-off channels are calibrated per-epoch (off_from support is in baonoise master); their operating points live in the companion policy brief.

Detector configuration

  • Schema pilotproxy_detector_datatrawl_v3, detector pilot-proxy/0.3.0.dev0, kernel 2.1.0, contract pilotproxy_chime_detector_contract_v1.
  • Statistic F = 2 Ptarget / (Pref,lower + Pref,upper), references at ±2 coarse bins with 1 guard bin. Each frame sums 2048 input streams × 128 detector windows of 128 samples (41.9 ms per frame); μ0 from exact integer weight norms. Fine reduction: time-coherent FFT along the window axis, 256 envelope bins of 11.92 Hz (pad 2), CFAR pFA = 10−3, int64-exact marginal identity enforced per frame.
  • uint64 power accumulators: zero overflows recorded in all 20 files. Reference placement nominal in all 20 (no DC-shifted, edge-wrapped, or forbidden-tone collisions).
  • Per-channel weight-bank and manifest SHA-256 recorded in every file; source hash pinned in the detector version string.
  • Median 3 frames per event-channel file (max 33); a few hundred units per channel contribute zero valid frames and are excluded from per-event statistics.
